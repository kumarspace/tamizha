Camera selection guide

Choose the Right CCTV Camera for Your Requirements

A quick overview of the main camera and recorder types, so you know what to ask about when you request a quotation.

Wired CCTV cameras

Analogue HD cameras connect to a DVR using coaxial cable. They are a widely used, cost-effective option for fixed positions around homes and shops.

IP cameras

IP cameras send digital video over network cable, often with power supplied through the same cable (PoE). They record to an NVR, and resolution and features depend on the model.

Wi-Fi cameras

Wi-Fi cameras connect over your wireless network and need fewer cables, which can suit small spaces. They still need power, and a stable Wi-Fi signal is essential for reliable viewing.

DVR recording systems

A Digital Video Recorder records footage from wired analogue HD cameras onto a hard disk, with playback on a monitor and, where configured, on a mobile phone.

NVR recording systems

A Network Video Recorder records IP cameras over the network. It is commonly chosen for larger systems or wherever network cameras are preferred.

Recording storage and playback

How long footage is kept depends on the number of cameras, the resolution, the recording mode and the hard disk capacity. Playback lets you search recordings by date and time.
